📜 Indian Railways Refund Rules

  • > 48 hours before departure: Flat clerkage charge deducted (₹120 for 1A/2A, ₹60 for SL). 90%+ refund.
  • 12 to 48 hours before departure: 25% of total ticket fare deducted (75% refundable).
  • 4 to 12 hours before departure: 50% of total ticket fare deducted (50% refundable).
  • < 4 hours or after Chart Prep: No refund admissible.
